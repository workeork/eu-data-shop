Method: the figures are the main published price line, as published by the hospitals. Hospitals often list several lines for one service (for example a facility fee and a separate doctor's fee). For each hospital and service we keep the highest-priced line (by list price, then cash price) and take the median negotiated rate across all payers within it. We then leave out values more than 5× the median of the other hospitals, and imaging values under $25. Left out as outliers: Trinity Health Ann Arbor (MRI brain, $1,248); Trinity Health Ann Arbor (CT abdomen & pelvis, $1,788); Henry Ford St John Hospital (Chest X-ray, $17). This approximates the main charge; it is not a total of all fees for an episode of care. Source: the hospitals' own machine-readable standard-charge files (45 CFR Part 180), files dated 2025-12 to 2026-06. Processed on 2026-10-14. Prices are in USD.
