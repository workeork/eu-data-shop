FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Only official public sources: TED (Tenders Electronic Daily, EU Publications Office), the European Commission Funding & Tenders Portal, RDW Open Data (Netherlands Vehicle Authority), and the machine-readable price files that US health plans must publish under the Transparency in Coverage rule (45 CFR 147.212). We are not affiliated with any of these bodies.

What am I paying for if the data is public?

For the work: finding the right files, filtering them to your criteria, and delivering a clean spreadsheet. US payer files are often hundreds of MB to tens of GB of nested JSON; we stream-parse them so you don't have to.

How do I pay?

After your order e-mail we send an invoice with payment details. Please quote your order number. You receive the file once payment is received; for repeat customers we can deliver first.

How reliable are the US price benchmarks?

Every number comes straight from the payer's own published file and carries its date. Placeholder rates ($0–$1) and expired contracts are removed. Percentage-of-charge rates are shown separately and never mixed with dollar amounts. Professional and facility rates are benchmarked separately. Provider names, cities and specialties come from the public CMS NPPES registry.

How fresh is the data?

EU and RDW data are queried live when we prepare your file. US payer files are updated monthly by the plans; we use the latest file and show its date in every row.

Can I get a free sample for my sector?

Yes. E-mail us your sector and countries (or payer and codes) and we will send a short sample.

Can I cancel the monthly digest?

Anytime: reply to any digest e-mail with "cancel". No further invoices are sent.

Do you store my data?

Only your e-mail and order details, to deliver and invoice the order. Order data is deleted after the legal retention period for invoices; we never sell or share it.

Need something specific?

Other countries, other codes, a recurring feed? Ask us and we'll quote within one business day.
